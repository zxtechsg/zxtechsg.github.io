07

Working principles

Clear rules.
Better work.

The audience’s trust is not a constraint. It is what makes the partnership valuable.

01

Creator-led. Partner-aligned.

ZX retains final editorial judgement over concept, structure, voice, and cut. Objectives, required facts, deliverables, and compliance needs are agreed before production.

02

A partnership never buys an opinion.

Brand messages and ZX’s independent assessment will not be presented as the same thing. Audience trust is the foundation of useful work.

03

Disclosure is clear and upfront.

Paid, gifted, affiliate, loaned, and other material relationships are labelled honestly, in line with platform rules and applicable guidance.

04

Talking points are a brief—not a script.

Partners can supply goals, approved claims, links, calls to action, and required facts. ZX may rewrite or reorder them for accuracy, clarity, and natural delivery.

05

Reviews stay focused.

Where included, partners may review an outline or script and provide up to two consolidated feedback rounds covering factual accuracy, agreed deliverables, and brand or legal requirements.

06

Extra rights are scoped separately.

Reposting, cross-posting, paid media, whitelisting, cutdowns, exclusivity, extended usage, and rush timelines are available by separate written agreement.
